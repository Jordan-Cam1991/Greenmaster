Smart Sprinkler Guidelines
🌧️
The 5mm Rain Skip Rule: If the forecast shows 5mm or more of rain, disable automatic sprinklers for 48 hours to protect against Pythium root rot.
🌅
Dawn Watering Window: Always irrigate between 4:30 AM and 7:00 AM. Evening watering keeps blades wet all night, inviting fungal spores.
