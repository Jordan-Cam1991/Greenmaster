# 🌱 GreenMaster AI - Precision Lawn Care & Multi-Horizon Planner

An intelligent, mobile-first precision lawn management progressive web app (PWA) built specifically for high-standard turf care enthusiasts, golf course greenkeepers, and bowling green caretakers.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

You can run **GreenMaster AI** immediately in any modern web browser:

### Option A: Local Server (Recommended)
Open your terminal in this directory and run:
```bash
python3 server.py
```
Then visit:
👉 **[http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080)**

### Option B: Direct File Open
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it directly in Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Brave.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Weekly Turf Vision AI (Photo Analysis)
- **Camera & Photo Upload**: Snap a photo of your turf directly from your smartphone or upload a file.
- **On-Device Agronomic Computer Vision**:
  - **DGCI (Dark Green Color Index)**: Normalized chlorophyll density measurement.
  - **Canopy Density %**: Detects bare soil vs dense stolon/rhizome coverage.
  - **Blade Cut Quality**: Analyzes whether blade tips are surgically sheared (sharp reel mower) or shredded (dull rotary).
  - **Weed & Discoloration Detection**: Identifies clover, dandelion, winter grass, or drought stress patches.
- **Privacy & Storage Friendly**:
  - *No heavy image files are stored on your device.* Only the lightweight numerical health score and diagnostic action plan are retained.
- **Instant Demo Turfs**:
  - Test the AI instantly using 4 built-in presets: **⛳ Golf Green (12mm Reel Cut)**, **☀️ Thirsty / Drought Stress**, **🌿 Weed Pressure**, and **🏡 Healthy Suburban**.
- **Optional Gemini Vision Integration**:
  - Built-in client and server support for Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision for deep natural-language agronomy diagnosis.

---

### 2. Dynamic 6-Horizon Action Planner
Every time you take a photo or adjust your profile, the AI updates a dynamic schedule across 6 distinct timeframes:

1. **Next Week (7 Days)**: Immediate precision actions (reel mowing frequency, morning irrigation timing, spot weed spray).
2. **Two Weeks (14 Days)**: Foliar spoon-feeding (liquid N + Chelated Iron), grooming reel pass, edge trenching.
3. **One Month (30 Days)**: Soil wetting agent application, microbial biostimulants, cylinder backlapping.
4. **One Quarter (3 Months)**: Pre-emergent chemical barrier (Prodiamine / Barricade), USGA topdressing sand levelling.
5. **Half Year (6 Months)**: Hollow tine core aeration, light scarifying, sprinkler precipitation audit.
6. **Full Year (12 Months)**: Spring master reset (scalp to ground, heavy dethatch, lab soil test, cylinder spin grind).

---

### 3. Comprehensive Maintenance Profile
Customize the app to your exact lawn setup:
- **Approach Standards**:
  - 🛋️ **Low Maintenance (Weekend Warrior)**: Monthly rotary mowing, slow-release granular feed, deep occasional soak.
  - 🏆 **Semi-Professional (Lawn Enthusiast)**: Striping pride, 20-30mm HOC, liquid spoon-feeding, pre-emergent prevention.
  - ⛳ **Full Professional (Golf Course / Bowling Green)**: Cylinder reel mowing sub-15mm (2-3x/week), PGR (Primo Maxx) regulation, USGA sand topdressing, foliar iron, wetting agents.
- **Grass Varieties**: Bermuda / Couch (TifTuf, Santa Ana), Buffalo / St Augustine, Zoysia, Kikuyu, Kentucky Bluegrass, Tall Fescue, Ryegrass.
- **Height of Cut (HOC)**: Exact mm adjustment with live 1/3rd cut rule guidance.
- **Irrigation**: In-ground MP Rotators, pop-up sprays, smart WiFi controllers, or hose sprinklers.
- **Equipment Checklist**: Reel/Cylinder mower, rotary with roller, scarifier/dethatcher, coring aerator, battery backpack sprayer, rotary spreader, sand levelling drag.
- **Chemicals Owned**: Pre-emergents, selective broadleaf herbicides, PGR, biostimulants, wetting agents.

---

### 4. Push Notifications & Calendar Sync
- **Web Push Notifications**: Automatic reminders for weekly photo scans, pre-emergent drop dates, and mowing cycles.
- **Audio Chimes**: Pleasant acoustic feedback when checking off maintenance tasks.
- **iCalendar (.ics) One-Click Sync**: Export your full 6-horizon AI care plan straight into **Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook**.
